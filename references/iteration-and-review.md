# Iteration and review

Generate a bounded set, normally 2–4 candidates per round. Score each candidate against the same brief:

- claim/brief fit;
- composition and usable negative space;
- subject correctness and geometry;
- absence of accidental text, logos and third-party marks;
- factual implication and rights safety;
- crop resilience at required ratios;
- visible generation defects.

Select, reject or revise with evidence. Every revision names a hypothesis such as “move the robot left to create headline space” or “reduce background detail for thumbnail legibility.” Keep rejected candidate records; do not regenerate endlessly without a decision criterion.
